RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is entered into on 15th February 2026 between Mr. Rajesh Khanna
(hereinafter "Landlord") and Ms. Ananya Verma (hereinafter "Tenant").

WHEREAS the Landlord is the lawful owner of the premises described herein and
agrees to let the same on lease to the Tenant on the terms and conditions set
forth below.

1. PREMISES: The Landlord agrees to rent Apartment 12C, Skyline Residency,
   Bengaluru 560001 to the Tenant for residential use only. The Tenant shall
   not use the premises for any commercial, professional, or illegal purpose.

2. TERM: The lease shall be for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing
   1st March 2026 and ending 28th January 2027. No renewal is automatic;
   renewal requires a fresh written agreement signed by both parties.

3. RENT: The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 42,000 (Rupees Forty-Two
   Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. A late fee
   of Rs. 100 per day shall apply for delayed payment beyond the 10th of the
   month. If rent remains unpaid for 15 consecutive days, the Landlord may
   terminate this agreement immediately.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT: The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of
   Rs. 84,000 (two months' rent). This deposit shall be refunded within 45
   days of vacation of premises, after deducting any unpaid dues, damage
   charges, or cost of repainting. The Landlord may deduct an amount equal to
   one month's rent for repainting if the Tenant has occupied the premises
   for more than 6 months.

5. TERMINATION: Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days'
   written notice. The Landlord may terminate immediately in case of:
   (a) non-payment of rent for 15 consecutive days,
   (b) illegal activities on the premises,
   (c) structural damage caused by the Tenant,
   (d) subletting without prior written consent.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION: Any disputes arising out of this agreement shall be
   resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996.
   The arbitral tribunal shall consist of a sole arbitrator appointed by the
   Landlord. The seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru. The courts at
   Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

7. GOVERNING LAW: This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and
   specifically the Transfer of Property Act 1882.

8. NO SUBLETTING: The Tenant shall not sublet, assign, or part with possession
   of the premises or any part thereof without prior written consent of the
   Landlord. Any such attempt shall be deemed a material breach.

9. MAINTENANCE: Minor repairs (under Rs. 1,500) shall be borne by the Tenant.
   Major repairs shall be the Landlord's responsibility, provided the Tenant
   has notified the Landlord in writing within 7 days of discovery.

10. INSPECTION: The Landlord reserves the right to inspect the premises with
    24 hours' prior notice, at reasonable hours of the day.

11. UTILITIES: Electricity, water, and internet charges shall be borne by
    the Tenant and paid directly to the respective service providers.

12. PETS: No pets are allowed on the premises without prior written consent.

13. MODIFICATIONS: Any modification to this agreement shall be in writing
    and signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have signed this agreement on the
date first above written.
